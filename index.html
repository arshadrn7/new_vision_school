A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Helpful answers.

Which classes does the school offer?

New Vision offers Classes 1 to 7, with primary education in Classes 1–5 and upper primary education in Classes 6 and 7.

What is the medium of instruction?

Teaching is in English, following the State Board pattern, with a focus on understanding concepts and building communication skills.

How can I apply for admission?

Send an admission enquiry below or visit our school in Utnoor. The school team can confirm seat availability, fees, eligibility, and the documents needed for your child’s class.

When can we visit the school?

School hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM–4:00 PM. Please call Shaik Rayees Sir on +91 9490357868 to arrange a visit and confirm availability, especially around holidays.
